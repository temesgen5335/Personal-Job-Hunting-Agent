# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and circumstances.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Remember that this is a spare-time project — people owe you nothing, and patience
  costs you little.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private
- Discriminatory or derogatory language of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report to **temesgen5335@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may
edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this policy, and may
block accounts for repeated or severe violations.

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short deliberately — a policy nobody reads protects nobody.
